# APRIL ideation prompt bank

Adapt freely; replace bracketed slots.

## Topic brainstorm
List 5 researchable angles on [phenomenon] for [population/context], each with a one-sentence contribution claim and a feasibility note (data/access).

## Research questions
Rewrite these aims as answerable RQs. Prefer open RQs for qualitative work and specific, testable RQs/hypotheses for confirmatory quantitative work: [aims].

## Hypotheses
For each RQ, draft H0/H1 in plain British English, naming constructs and expected direction: [RQs].

## Scope fence
What must this paper include, and what should be deferred to a follow-up study, given a [6000–8000]-word journal article?
