# april-01-ideation

APRIL — Academic Research Skills by April.

## Goal
Turn a vague interest into a workable research agenda: topic → gap → RQs/hypotheses → scope boundaries.

## Workflow
1. Clarify audience (journal field), contribution type, and constraints (data access, timeline).
2. Surface 2–3 candidate angles; stress-test novelty and feasibility.
3. Draft research questions (qual, quan, or mixed) and, if confirmatory, hypotheses.
4. State inclusions/exclusions and key constructs/definitions.
5. Hand off to `april-02-literature` or `april-03-methodology` as needed.

## Outputs
Topic statement; gap statement; RQ list (± hypotheses); scope note; open questions.
