# Email guidance provenance

## Supplied source

- Author: Anya 在外企.
- Title: 职场最被高看的邮件风格是“Clear but warm”.
- Platform: Xiaohongshu / 小红书.
- [Canonical post](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/discovery/item/6ab5eedc0000000002028e55).
- Accessed: 27 September 2026. Reviewed the caption and 16 image cards through the browser; the user also supplied screenshots of the scenario and training passages.

The source recommends making the purpose easy to find while maintaining consideration for the recipient. It illustrates approval requests, follow-ups, refusals, bad news, and feedback. Its practice exercises are to isolate the essential message before adding warmth, check the first sentence, and study an effective colleague's email structure.

The skill paraphrases these ideas and does not reproduce the post's templates. The author's accounts of improved response rates and professional standing are personal experience, not evidence of a guaranteed outcome. Promotional resources in the final cards were not incorporated.

## User requirements

- Use clean, clear, warm writing whenever the user asks for an email.
- Write with less defensive wording.
- Make length and paragraph structure dynamic: no fixed three paragraphs or artificially similar paragraph lengths.
- Keep APRIL skills numbered consecutively and sync authorised updates between Codex and GitHub; remind the user about GitHub when a local skill changes.

## APRIL implementation decisions

The skill adds practical controls for factual accuracy, real commitments, recipient and language calibration, flexible output scope, and email-app actions. These are implementation safeguards rather than claims made by the post. Its scenarios and opening/closing examples are flexible heuristics; actual context and user instructions determine the final message.
