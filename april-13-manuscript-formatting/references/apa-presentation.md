# APA presentation baseline and April's house rules

Read the [standing manuscript policy](../../references/core/manuscript-house-style.md) for April's actual requirements. This reference distinguishes official APA 7 presentation from personal overrides; it is not a second house-style checklist. Explicit task decisions and applicable journal requirements govern exceptions.

## Headings and font

APA has **five** heading levels, using only as many as the hierarchy needs. Do not impose a maximum of three or skip levels. Its baseline is title case, no heading numbering, double spacing and no extra blank lines. [Official headings guidance](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/headings).

| Level | APA baseline |
|---|---|
| 1 | Centred, bold, separate paragraph |
| 2 | Flush left, bold, separate paragraph |
| 3 | Flush left, bold italic, separate paragraph |
| 4 | Indented, bold, ending with a full stop; text continues on the same line |
| 5 | Indented, bold italic, ending with a full stop; text continues on the same line |

Twelve-point Times New Roman is an accepted APA font. April has selected that uniform 12-point baseline for the title, body, headings and page numbers; the policy applies it throughout manuscript text. APA does not prescribe the tentative enlarged 16/14/12-point ladder. Within figure images APA recommends sans serif 8–14 pt; April instead requires Times New Roman. [Official font guidance](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/font).

April's **all-left heading alignment** overrides centred Level 1. Levels 4/5 retain APA's run-in bold/bold-italic structure and 0.5-inch first-line indentation: paragraph alignment remains left, so these are compatible. Use distinct semantic heading levels even where Levels 1/2 look similar under the house preference. Her latest manuscript choice is APA title case; preserve unrelated prose/global preferences. Her named Introduction also overrides APA's usual opening under the repeated paper title.

## Page and paragraph defaults

When no user/journal exception applies: use [1-inch margins](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/margins), [double spacing without added paragraph spacing](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/line-spacing), left-aligned ragged-right body text and 0.5-inch first-line paragraph indents. The abstract's first line is not indented; references use 0.5-inch hanging indents. [Paragraph-format guidance](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/paragraph-format).

APA's [page order](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/order-pages) places references before appendices and allows tables/figures embedded after first mention. April's grouped front matter, explicit page break before Introduction, six main sections, discussion-contained implications/limitations/contributions, sole top-right automatic page number and no other header/footer content remain her standing requirements. Do not add a professional running head against her instruction.

## Tables, figures and appendices

APA uses a bold table/figure number above an italic title-case title, with notes below **only when needed**. Apply the policy's minimum-necessary-note rule; do not remove essential interpretation details or required attribution. Table bodies may use single, 1.5 or double spacing for readability; number, title and notes remain double spaced. [Tables](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/tables-figures/tables), [figures](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/tables-figures/figures).

Sparse horizontal rules are consistent with APA; April's three-line, black-on-white table default has the justified exceptions stated in her policy. It is not a claim that every APA table has exactly three rules.

Each [appendix](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/appendices) starts on a new final page, with label and title on separate lines, and is referred to in the text. Appendix citations belong in the main reference list. April's left-alignment preference overrides APA's centred appendix labels/titles.

## Reporting is a different layer

[APA JARS](https://apastyle.apa.org/jars) concerns what research information is reported, not font sizes or heading design. Use the suite's [reporting router](../../references/reporting/reporting-router.md) for Quant, Qual, Mixed and applicable modules. Flag reporting omissions for the author; never invent methods/results or silently restructure substantive content during formatting.
