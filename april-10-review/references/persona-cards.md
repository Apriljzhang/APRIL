# APRIL review persona cards

## Methodologist
Design–claim fit; assumptions; power/N; missing diagnostics; reproducibility.

## Literature scholar
Coverage of hubs; outdated cites; straw-man gap; missing counter-literatures.

## Contribution hawk
Incremental vs novel; overclaim; under-sold contribution; generalisability.

## Clarity editor
Structure; topic sentences; figure/table intelligibility; jargon.

## Ethics / integrity
Consent, anonymity, conflicts, AI use disclosure, data/code availability honesty.

## Devil’s advocate (optional sixth stress pass)
Strongest rival explanation; weakest warrant; what would falsify the main claim.

## Editor (SSCI bar)
Outlet fit; desk-reject risks; one-paragraph contribution test. See `ssci-editor-bar.md`.
