# april-05-discussion

## Scope control (mandatory)

Read `../references/core/operating-contract.md` before acting. Interpret only the requested locked findings or Discussion component; do not draft the rest of the manuscript or run new analyses.

Read `../references/core/manuscript-contract.md` to preserve the study's claim boundaries and `../references/evidence/evidence-integrity.md` before connecting findings to sources.

## Goal
Interpret findings against prior work without overclaiming.

## RQ-ordered organisation

For a complete Discussion, follow the stated research questions in their original order. Use RQ-focused descriptive subsection headings with the house policy's hierarchical decimal numbering, for example 5.1 and 5.2 when Discussion is section 5. The numbers illustrate the actual outline position, not a requirement that Discussion always be section 5; the journal-adapted version follows its required numbering. With one RQ, or an article framed by aims rather than explicit RQs, use the actual question/aims and do not invent extra questions or equal-length subsections. This describes the full-section architecture, not permission to expand a request for one component.

Choose one concise orientation strategy: a brief synthesis of the key findings before the RQ subsections, **or** a short findings summary answering the relevant RQ at each subsection opening. Avoid duplicating a full overview and the same summaries in every subsection; retain only the empirical reminders needed for interpretation.

Within each RQ discussion, move from the verified answer/finding to its interpretation, relevant literature/theory, explanation of convergence or divergence, and the evidence-bounded contribution. Keep this study's findings active; do not reproduce the Results or restart the Literature Review. Explain what the comparison with prior work changes or clarifies rather than displaying citations. Distinguish a tested explanation from a plausible interpretation, and calibrate causal/general claims to the design.

After the RQ-ordered interpretation, organise the remaining material within Discussion in this order:

1. **Research/theoretical contributions and implications:** synthesise what the findings collectively add, refine or qualify. Combine overlapping contribution/implication claims instead of repeating them.
2. **Practical implications:** specify who could do what and the evidence-based conditions or limits.
3. **Policy implications, when warranted:** include only where the findings support a credible policy connection; omit an inapplicable category rather than inventing relevance.
4. **Limitations:** explain concrete limits on interpretation, inference or transferability.
5. **Future research:** tie feasible next questions or designs to those limits, unresolved findings or explanations, rather than provide a generic wish list.

These components stay inside Discussion. Any separate Conclusion closes the paper without new evidence; combine Discussion and Conclusion only when expressly selected or required. Do not fabricate references, findings or policy claims to fill this outline.

## Workflow
1. Orient the reader within the RQ-ordered structure above, restating key findings concisely in plain British English (no new analyses). Keep the study's current findings visibly active throughout the section: each interpretive paragraph should be anchored in a specific verified pattern, theme, contrast, or participant account from this study.
2. For each major finding: empirical anchor → literature link → explanation or warrant → boundary conditions → contribution or implication, using only the moves supported by the evidence. Avoid paragraphs that drift into a generic literature review or mention a finding only in passing.
   - Give each paragraph a topic sentence that states its interpretive claim. Use subsequent sentences to develop that claim through study evidence, comparison with prior research, explanation, qualification, alternatives, or implications. The order is flexible, but the logic must remain traceable.
   - Maintain cohesion across paragraphs by signalling whether the next paragraph extends, qualifies, contrasts with, or integrates the previous interpretation. Vary paragraph length according to the interpretive work; do not force uniformity or add filler.
3. Use Discussion **phrase functions** in `../references/rhetoric/discussion-moves.md` (APA Discussion Phrases Guide–inspired) as selectable rhetorical moves within the organisation above. Their reference-list order is not the required order of subsections.
4. When a literature connection requires page-accurate evidence, read `../references/evidence/pdf-quote-search.md` and use APRIL's integrated local locator. Do not invoke a separate PDF quote-finder skill.
5. Optional Zotero search (`pz search`) to locate items in the user’s library.
6. Integrate relevant alternative explanations with the interpretation; organise warranted contributions/implications, limitations and future research in the sequence above.
7. Read `../references/reporting/reporting-router.md`; check the Discussion items in the complete applicable Quant, Qual, or Mixed pack, plus relevant REC items.
8. Optional Toulmin structure for contested claims (claim–data–warrant–backing–qualifier–rebuttal).
9. Use relevant `april-08-language` guidance within the authorised Discussion work for hedging, natural language dynamics, and Phrasebank discussing-findings moves; do not automatically launch a separate manuscript-wide editing pass. Ensure the closing move states the study's bounded contribution and hands a clear take-away to any separate Conclusion without adding evidence.

## Optional narrative enhancement

When the user asks for stronger storytelling or narrative depth, read `../references/rhetoric/empirical-storytelling.md`. A useful optional sequence is empirical answer → literature relation → tested mechanism or proposed explanation → evidenced condition/boundary → alternative explanation → contribution. Discuss a surprising, contradictory, null, or deviant pattern only when it genuinely changes the interpretation. These moves are not compulsory Discussion elements.

## Integrity
Never invent citations or pages. Do not introduce new results.

For cross-sectional network findings, describe edges as model-dependent conditional associations. Do not recast central, predictable, or bridge nodes as causes, mechanisms, or intervention targets. Treat a nonsignificant NCT as failure to detect a difference, not proof that networks are equal.
