# APRIL - SEM and CFA

## When
Latent measurement or simultaneous structural relations are central. Route observed-variable indirect/conditional effects to `mediation-moderation.md`; use both cards for mediation in SEM.

## Workflow
1. Specify constructs, indicators, scaling, identification, residual correlations, cross-loadings, and paths from theory.
2. Use data type, distribution, clustering, missingness, and sample size to choose estimator/correction; do not default ordinal indicators to normal-theory ML.
3. Establish measurement adequacy before structural interpretation, or justify one-step modeling. Test required measurement invariance for group/longitudinal comparisons.
4. Evaluate global and local fit using chi-square, CFI/TLI, RMSEA with interval, SRMR, residuals, and parameter plausibility. Cutoffs are contextual diagnostics, not universal laws.
5. Report unstandardized estimates with uncertainty and label standardization. Include relevant loadings, variances, covariances, indirect effects, and explained variance.
6. Pre-specify or label respecification. Do not add paths solely from modification indices; validate meaningful revisions where possible.

## Reporting
Give model, estimator, missing-data method, identification, software/version, convergence, fit, focal parameters, respecifications, and sensitivities. Follow JARS-Quant SEM.

## Core references
Use `../references/method-citations.md`: Hu and Bentler (1999), Enders and Bandalos (2001), and Rhemtulla et al. (2012).
