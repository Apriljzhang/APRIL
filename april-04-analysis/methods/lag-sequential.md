# APRIL - Lag sequential analysis

## When
Coded behavioural or interaction events are ordered in time and the question concerns transitions or contingencies.

## Workflow
1. Define unit, event/interval coding, code co-occurrence, sequence boundaries, lag/time window, and coder reliability.
2. Preserve structural zeros and explain ties, simultaneous events, missing segments, and interrupted sequences.
3. Report transition counts and conditional probabilities against relevant base rates.
4. Assess whether pooling people, sessions, or phases is defensible; examine heterogeneity and stationarity.
5. Match inference to dependence and sparse cells. If Allison-Liker z or Yule's Q is used, state its assumptions and limits; consider log-linear, randomization, permutation, or model-based alternatives.
6. Address multiplicity and interpret sequential dependence without causal overclaim.

## Reporting
Provide codebook, reliability, lag definition, sequence count, transition table, effect measure, uncertainty/test, multiplicity rule, and reproducible script.
