# APRIL APA caption notes

Read the [standing manuscript policy](../../references/core/manuscript-house-style.md) for the actual table/figure number, title, alignment, font and minimum-necessary-note requirements. Use [the APA presentation reference](apa-presentation.md) to distinguish APA defaults from house overrides and journal adaptations.

Check that every table/figure is numbered in order and called out in the text, with a brief descriptive title and only the notes needed for independent interpretation. Do not manufacture a note or remove necessary definitions, statistical information or attribution to force a fixed caption length.
