# april-08-language

APRIL — Academic Paper Research & Inquiry Lab.

## Order of operations
1. **House style gate (authoritative)** — rules below.
2. **Humanizer pass** — load `references/humanizer_academic/SKILL.md`.
3. **Conflict rule:** house style > humanizer.

## House style rules (authoritative)
- Use **British English**.
- Use **academic English**.
- **No em dashes**.
- **No 'not x but y' pattern**.
- **Long short, simple complex compound sentences are mixedly used in the writing.**
- Use **simple language** in the writing.
- Use the **Manchester phrase bank** to adapt their speech patterns.
- Use **topic sentence, then supporting evidence** (with evidence, explanation, example etc.) to make the argumentation.
- Or use the **Toulmin Model** to write up the argumentation (claim, data, warrant, backing, qualifier, rebuttal).
