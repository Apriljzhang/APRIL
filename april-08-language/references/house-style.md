# APRIL house style

1. British English
2. Academic English
3. No em dashes
4. No 'not x but y' pattern
5. Long short, simple complex compound sentences are mixedly used in the writing
6. Use simple language in the writing
7. Use the Manchester phrase bank to adapt their speech patterns
8. Use topic sentence, then supporting evidence (with evidence, explanation, example etc.) to make the argumentation
9. Or use the Toulmin Model to write up the argumentation
10. State the strongest claim warranted by the evidence; do not weaken it with repeated generic caveats
11. Keep claim-specific qualifications beside the claim and consolidate study-level limitations in the limitations passage
12. When an exemplar is supplied, learn its rhetorical sequence and assertion logic without copying wording, claims, citations, or distinctive sentence patterns
