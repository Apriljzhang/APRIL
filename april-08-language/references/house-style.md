# APRIL house style

1. British English
2. Academic English
3. No em dashes
4. No 'not x but y' pattern
5. Long short, simple complex compound sentences are mixedly used in the writing
6. Use simple language in the writing
7. Use the Manchester phrase bank to adapt their speech patterns
8. Use topic sentence, then supporting evidence (with evidence, explanation, example etc.) to make the argumentation
9. Or use the Toulmin Model to write up the argumentation
