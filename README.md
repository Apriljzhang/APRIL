# APRIL — Academic Paper Research & Inquiry Lab

**APRIL** helps you plan, write, and revise academic journal papers—from research questions through analysis, discussion, language polish, formatting, peer-style review, and revision.

It works as a **staged skill suite**: load one stage at a time so the agent focuses on the requested part of the manuscript instead of generating a whole paper in one shot. Shared contracts and reference families live in `references/` as APRIL Commons; they are internal resources, not extra skills.

## What APRIL helps with

- Turning a vague topic into clear problems, purposes, and research questions  
- Searching and synthesising literature (including Zotero and local PDF quotes)  
- Choosing and justifying methods, then running analysis with the right method guide  
- Writing Discussion, Introduction, Conclusion, and Abstract  
- Polishing British English academic style  
- Checking APA formatting and reporting completeness (APA Style JARS)  
- Stress-testing the draft and preparing a response to reviewers  

## Defaults (change if your journal asks)

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Article length | 6000–8000 words |
| Abstract | 200–300 words |
| Font / spacing | Times New Roman 12pt, double-spaced |
| References | APA |
| Captions | APA two-line style |
| Language | British English |

## How to use

1. Open the APRIL stage that matches what you need right now.  
2. For analysis, also open **one** method card under `april-04-analysis/methods/`.  
3. Move to the next stage when that part of the paper is stable.
