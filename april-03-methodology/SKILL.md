# april-03-methodology

APRIL — Academic Research Skills by April.

## Goal
Decide **whether** a design fits the RQs; critique weaknesses; produce design diagrams. Execution → `april-04-analysis`.

## Workflow
1. Restate RQs and claim type.
2. Map to design family.
3. For person-centred / longitudinal / text goals, choose among LPA vs LCA, CLPM vs RI-CLPM, STM vs qualitative coding, etc.
4. List assumptions, sampling, measures, procedures, validity threats.
5. Specify diagrams; hand off to one `april-04` method card.
